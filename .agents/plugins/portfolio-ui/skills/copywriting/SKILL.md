# Copywriting: High-Impact Personal Brand & Marketing Copy

Use this skill to craft clear, memorable, and conversion-oriented copy across portfolio bios, hero taglines, project narratives, and social announcements.

---

## 1. The Personal Brand Value Proposition

Every personal site headline should answer 3 questions in under 5 seconds:
1. **Who are you?** (e.g. *NUS Business Analytics final-year student*)
2. **What is your edge / superpower?** (e.g. *Specializing in Agentic AI & Machine Learning*)
3. **What tangible impact do you deliver?** (e.g. *Building local-first multi-agent systems and predictive risk pipelines at scale*)

### The "No-Fluff" Hero Formula
```text
[Identity & Focus]: "Final-year Business Analytics undergrad at NUS specializing in Machine Learning."
[Superpower / Proof]: "Building data-driven AI solutions and predictive models that solve complex, real-world problems at scale."
```

## 3. Case Study Storytelling: The ARC Framework

Structure each project case study like an engineering documentary:
1. **The Agony (Problem)**: Define the concrete friction. Who was suffering, what was slow, what was breaking?
   - *Example*: "Clinicians needed deep insights from OMOP CDM data, but writing complex SQL queries was a major bottleneck, and cloud LLMs risked leaking patient health records."
2. **The Relief (Solution)**: State the architectural breakthrough concisely.
   - *Example*: "Engineered ANCHOR, an autonomous 8-agent LangGraph system operating locally in an 8 GB VRAM footprint with zero patient data leakage."
3. **The Proof (Metrics & Outcome)**: Provide 3 hard numbers that validate success.
   - *Example*: "Zero data leakage, 100% schema conformance, under 8 GB VRAM utilization."

---

## 5. Copywriting Quality Checklist
- [ ] Eliminated cliché filler words ("passionate about", "synergy", "hardworking team player").
- [ ] Replaced abstract adjectives with concrete nouns and verbs ("orchestrated 8 agents" vs "helped build AI").
- [ ] Short sentences (avg 12–18 words) with varied sentence lengths for natural cadence.
- [ ] Clear call-to-action buttons ("View Case Study →", "Download Resume PDF").
